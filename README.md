# RSS reader

The latest stories from your RSS and Atom feeds, in one list, for
[EdgeStation](https://github.com/chainlist/edgestation).

- **Add a feed** with the **+** button: paste the address of a feed, or of a news site or blog
  that names its feed in its page. Or tap one of the suggested feeds.
- All feeds show in one list, newest first, with a picture when the feed gives one. Tap a feed's
  name at the top to show only that feed. **Remove feed** shows next to it.
- **Tap a story** to open its page in your default browser.

The feeds load when the page first shows, then at most every 15 minutes while the page is on
screen. Each placement keeps its own feeds.
